## The Time Zone Advantage You Are Missing

One of the most underappreciated benefits of hiring Ethiopian and East African developers is the time zone alignment.

Ethiopia operates in **GMT+3**, which means:

- **Full overlap** with the entire European workday
- **4–5 hours of crossover** with US East Coast business hours
- **Same-day collaboration** for most sprint ceremonies, code reviews, and standups

Compare this to hiring in Southeast Asia, where a US-based team often loses 12+ hours of overlap. African talent bridges that gap decisively.
