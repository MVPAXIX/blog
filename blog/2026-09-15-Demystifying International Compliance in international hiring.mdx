      <sub_headline>The implications of misclassification of full-time staff as independent contractors are high on the legal and financial level in various African jurisdictions.</sub_headline>
      <body>Foreign entities must navigate local labor definitions and tax structures carefully to avoid regulatory penalties[cite: 7].</body>
      <list_item>Be mindful of differences between local contractor/employee: Local statutory employment status may be triggered by worker hours, control levels and exclusivity, so foreign entities should take care of this distinction to ensure full international tech compliance[cite: 7].</list_item>
      <list_item>Assist with cross-border tax compliance: Reduce permanent establishment exposure and provide for proper withholding and social security matters[cite: 7].</list_item>
      <list_item>Make use of Employer of Record (EOR) options: Work with niche platforms to support borderless hiring and employ workers remotely without setting up a costly regional company[cite: 7].</list_item>

      <sub_headline>Securing proprietary source code and ensuring compliance with privacy laws and regulations across the world is a must if you're hiring overseas.</sub_headline>

      <list_item>Have a strong IP assignment process: Establish contracts to ensure that all work product and IP generated by remote engineers is immediately assigned to the primary entity[cite: 7].</list_item>
      <list_item>Ensure alignment with global data regulations: Ensure data pipelines and remote access methods meet global standards like GDPR as well as local data protection requirements[cite: 7].</list_item>
